01The method

One honest tap a day.

No numbers to type, no essays to write. You pick the picture that matches your day — and that's it.

  1. STEP 1

    Answer one question

    How was today? Tap one of five. Add a short note if the day deserves it.

  2. STEP 2

    Watch the month fill in

    Every day becomes a colored tile. Streaks grow — and forgive the odd miss.

  3. STEP 3

    See the patterns

    Weeks later, the picture is there: what lifts your days, and what drains them.

Why just one tap?

Because the habit is the hard part. A five-second check-in survives busy weeks, sick days and holidays — and an honest rating every day beats a detailed entry once a month. Patterns only appear when the data keeps coming.

When you have more to say, there's a note. When you don't, a tap is a complete entry.

02Five areas

Five petals, five areas.

Look closely at the logo — its five petals are the five things vivevo tracks. Each area has its own five-step scale, drawn in pictures instead of numbers.

  • Mood

    rain cloud → confetti

  • Energy

    empty battery → charging

  • Productivity

    paused → rocket launch

  • Diet

    cake → carrot

  • Relationship

    heartbreak → whole heart

Track one area or all five — each keeps its own streak, calendar and charts, and the whole app recolors to the area you're in.

03Staying with it

A streak that forgives.

Consistency should feel encouraging, not fragile. vivevo is built to keep you going — gently.

The flame never stops evolving.

Most streak counters are just a number. vivevo's flame keeps shifting color for five hundred days — and starts to glow from day 30.

day 1 90 180 365 500+

And a day you haven't rated yet never breaks the streak — the day gets to finish first.

  • Automatic streak freezes

    Miss a day and vivevo spends a freeze for you — no button to press. They refill every month.

  • Reminders with manners

    One daily nudge, only if you haven't checked in yet — and it quietly persists if you drift away.

  • Question of the day

    A small reflection that knows the clock: “What will make you smile today?” becomes “What made you smile?” by evening.

  • Quick check-in

    All five areas on one screen — straight from the reminder notification.

04The payoff

Patterns you can actually see.

Six charts, any date range, one question: what actually moves your days?
